About Zymovo

Zymovo is a biotechnology company dedicated to engineering biology to solve some of the world's most pressing challenges. Founded by a team of leading scientists and engineers, we are pioneering new approaches to drug discovery, development, and manufacturing.

Our mission is to translate breakthrough science into transformative medicines and sustainable technologies. We are committed to building a world-class organization that fosters innovation, collaboration, and a culture of excellence.
